# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Software engineers, computer science students, and interview candidates actively preparing for technical coding interviews at top tech companies.

## Product Purpose

CompanyWise provides curated, company-wise LeetCode problem collections mapped directly to technical interview loops. It enables developers to focus their preparation on the questions companies actually ask, track solving progress, and target high-frequency problem sets across 490 top tech companies.

## Positioning

Curated company-wise LeetCode problem mapping across 490 companies and 3,411 unique questions across 1,112 datasets (38,825 processed records) with frequency rankings and progress tracking, eliminating guessing about what companies ask in technical loops.

## Operating Context

Web browser desktop and mobile study sessions, split-screen coding alongside LeetCode, targeted multi-company interview preparation sprints.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 490 company problem sets with frequency rankings
- 3,411 curated unique LeetCode problems with difficulty, acceptance rate, and direct problem links
- 1,112 CSV datasets covering 38,825 processed records across 6 time periods (30 Days, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years, All Time)
- Multi-dimensional filtering by company, difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), acceptance rate, time period, and title search
- Interactive progress tracking: Solved, Attempted, and Unsolved states with local persistence, date-accurate practice activity tracking, and LeetCode public profile sync
- Dual-theme support: Light (`#F7F8FB`) and Warm Charcoal Dark (`#262624`) with developer-focused styling
- Dataset integrity: Original CSV files are preserved without modification

## Brand Commitments

- Name: CompanyWise
- Logo: Midnight Navy / White Squircle with three progression bars and Warm Amber peak dot; interactive ecosystem portal
- Tone: Focused, developer-centric, minimal, high-utility, zero decorative clutter

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified build report in `public/data/report.json`: 490 companies, 3,411 unique questions, 1,112 CSV files, 38,825 records
- Full company datasets in `public/data/` (pre-generated lightweight JSON per company)
- Verified SVG and PNG logos in `public/logos/`

## Product Principles

1. High-density utility over decorative filler
2. Precision tracking: exact percentages without misleading round-downs or fake data
3. Seamless contrast and readability across light and dark modes
4. Instant client-side search and filtering
5. Transparent synchronization: accurately separates public LeetCode submission imports from full lifetime solve metrics
