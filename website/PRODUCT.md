# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Software engineers, computer science students, and interview candidates actively preparing for technical coding interviews at top tech companies.

## Product Purpose
CompanyWise provides curated, company-wise LeetCode problem collections mapped directly to technical interview loops. It enables developers to focus their preparation on the questions companies actually ask, track solving progress, and target high-frequency problem sets across 490 top tech companies.

## Positioning
Curated company-wise LeetCode problem mapping across 490 companies and 3,411 questions with frequency rankings and progress tracking, eliminating guessing about what companies ask in technical loops.

## Operating Context
Web browser desktop and mobile study sessions, split-screen coding alongside LeetCode, targeted multi-company interview preparation sprints.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 490 company problem sets with frequency rankings
- 3,411 curated LeetCode problems with difficulty, acceptance rate, and direct problem links
- Multi-dimensional filtering by company, difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard), acceptance rate, and title search
- Interactive progress tracking: Solved, Attempted, and Unsolved states with local persistence and LeetCode account sync
- Dual-theme support: Light (`#FAFAFA`) and Dark (`#0A0A0B`) with developer-focused styling
- Dataset integrity: Original CSV files are preserved without modification

## Brand Commitments
- Name: CompanyWise
- Logo: Midnight Navy / White Squircle with three progression bars and Warm Amber peak dot
- Tone: Focused, developer-centric, minimal, high-utility, zero decorative clutter

## Evidence on Hand
- Full company datasets in `public/data/` (3,411 questions across 490 companies)
- Verified SVG and dark PNG logos in `public/logos/`

## Product Principles
1. High-density utility over decorative filler
2. Precision tracking: exact percentages without misleading round-downs or fake data
3. Seamless contrast and readability across light and dark modes
4. Instant client-side search and filtering
